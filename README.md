# GCD — Doctoral Enrollment Management System

**A digital solution for managing doctoral enrollment and annual re-enrollment at Université Sidi Mohamed Ben Abdellah (USMBA), Fez, Morocco.**

## Overview

GCD is a web application project designed to simplify and digitize administrative procedures related to doctoral studies. It focuses on two essential processes: **initial doctoral enrollment** and **annual re-enrollment**.

The project aims to provide a centralized environment where applicants, doctoral students, thesis supervisors, academic committees, and administrative staff can manage submissions, review documents, monitor academic progress, and follow enrollment decisions.

The workflows are designed with reference to Morocco's national doctoral training standards, particularly Ministerial Decision No. 1886.23 of August 1, 2024. Institution-specific procedures remain subject to validation against USMBA's official administrative requirements.

## Objectives

- Digitize doctoral enrollment and re-enrollment workflows.
- Reduce repetitive paperwork and manual administrative processing.
- Improve document management and validation tracking.
- Facilitate communication between doctoral students, supervisors, committees, and administrative services.
- Improve the traceability and transparency of enrollment decisions.
- Provide a clear overview of application status and required actions.

## Main Features

### 1. Initial Doctoral Enrollment

- Online submission of doctoral applications.
- Upload and management of application documents.
- Tracking of application status and review progress.
- Recording of admission decisions and candidate results.
- Management of the administrative enrollment process and National Thesis Charter.

### 2. Annual Re-enrollment

- Access to a personal doctoral student space.
- Submission of annual research progress reports.
- Upload and verification of required documents.
- Review of progress reports by the thesis supervisor and relevant academic committee.
- Tracking of re-enrollment approval and administrative completion.

### 3. User Roles and Access

The system is intended to support distinct roles with appropriate permissions:

- **Applicants:** submit applications and track admission progress.
- **Doctoral students:** manage their academic information, submit progress reports, and request re-enrollment.
- **Thesis supervisors:** review research progress and provide assessments.
- **Academic committees:** participate in the review and approval process where required.
- **Administrative staff:** manage records, verify submissions, and process enrollment procedures.

Actual permissions and approval responsibilities will be defined according to the institution's validated procedures.

### 4. Workflow Automation

The proposed system may support:

- Notifications about deadlines, missing documents, and status changes.
- Document completeness checks.
- Structured approval workflows.
- A history of actions and validation decisions.
- Administrative dashboards and progress monitoring.

These automation capabilities depend on the final implementation and institutional requirements.

## Workflow Design

The project is based on two principal activity diagrams:

**Initial Enrollment**

Application submission → Document review → Academic selection and admission decision → Required approvals and formalities → Administrative enrollment.

**Annual Re-enrollment**

Re-enrollment request → Progress report submission → Supervisor review → Committee review where applicable → Decision and administrative processing.

These diagrams represent the intended process structure; detailed steps may vary according to the university's official procedures.

## Regulatory Reference

**Reference:** Moroccan national doctoral training standards, Ministerial Decision No. 1886.23, dated August 1, 2024.

The reference framework addresses admission, doctoral registration, annual re-registration, and monitoring of thesis progress. In particular, annual re-registration involves a detailed progress report prepared by the thesis supervisor and reviewed through the applicable academic process.

GCD is an academic software project inspired by this framework. It is not an official USMBA platform, and the application does not replace university regulations or administrative decisions.
